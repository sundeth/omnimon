Main Settings

  • Ruleset: Defines the type of mini-game used in the module's Adventure Mode and the way traited eggs are obtained. Also defines the bonuses for power. dmc
  • Traited Egg Starting Level: Initial level for pets hatched from traited eggs. Higher levels provide stat bonuses including increased HP, power, and attack values. 3
  • Name Format: Template string for pet folder names. '$' is replaced with the pet's name. Example: "$_dmc" creates folders like "Agumon_dmc". Used for sprite loading paths. $_dmc
  • Visible Stats: Controls which statistics are displayed in the game UI. Allows customization of the information shown to players during gameplay. Age, Weight, Hunger, Strength, Effort and 6 more
  • Primary Sprite Format: The primary sprite format to load. Color uses 'monsters', Dot uses 'monsters_dot', HD uses 'monsters_hidef'. Falls back to secondary then the remaining format. Dot
  • Secondary Sprite Format: The secondary sprite format to try if primary is not found. Falls back to the remaining unselected format last. Color
  • Enable Special Attack Sprite: When enabled, critical attacks will use the 'Special' frame effect. No
  • Count Evolution While Sleeping: When enabled, evolution counters will increase even while the pet is sleeping. When disabled, evolution progress only occurs during awake time. No
  • Reverse Attack Frames: Swaps attack animation frames (ATK1↔ATK2, TRAIN1↔TRAIN2) during sprite loading. Used for visual variety or correcting animation sequences in sprite sets. No

Training Settings

  • Battle Minigame: The battle minigame type used during battles. Dummy Bar
  • Battle Protocol: The battle protocol is used to simulate a real device while connecting with non Omnipet devices. When set, Omnipet will emulate the selected device's communication protocol during battles. DM20
  • Effort Gain: Effort points gained per training session. At 16+ effort, pets gain significant power bonuses. Critical for unlocking evolution paths. 1
  • Strength Win: Strength increase from winning training sessions. Higher values accelerate pet strengthening. 1
  • Strength Lose: Strength increase from training sessions when losing. Usually less than winning gain. Helps maintain strength levels even when training performance is poor. 0
  • Strength Multiplier: Multiplier applied to strength gains from training based on performance. Higher performance in training yields extra strength points. 1
  • Weight Win: Weight loss when winning training. Helps manage pet weight and body condition. High weight can make a pet sick. 1
  • Weight Lose: Weight loss when losing training. Usually less than winning loss. Provides alternative weight management even when training performance is poor. 1

Battle Settings

  • Adventure Mode: Enables or disables adventure mode gameplay features. Required to add the module's Adventure Mode to the Battle Menu. Yes
  • Adventure Style: The Adventure Style defines if the player can only move to the next battle or reset progress (like the DM20) or if the player can revisit any defeated area freely. Next and Reset
  • Battle Cost Type: Which resource is required to battle. DP
  • Battle Cost Amount: The amount of the battle resource will be consumed when battling. 1
  • Base Sick Chance Win: Base percentage chance of getting sick after winning a battle. Modified by protein overdose and disturbance penalties. Minimum 5%, maximum 50%. 20
  • Base Sick Chance Lose: Base percentage chance of getting sick after losing a battle. Modified by protein overdose (+10% per overdose) and disturbance penalties. Higher risk than winning. 10
  • Attribute Advantage: Percentage advantage when pet's attribute matches favorably against enemy (Data > Virus > Vaccine > Data). Applied as damage multiplier in battle calculations. 5
  • Global Hit Points: When > 0, make the module ignore pets individual Health Points. It's also used to cap the possible damage taken. This attribute is mostly obsolete with the protocol battle system. 4
  • Sequential Rounds: Controls battle area progression mechanics. When enabled, battles progress through rounds sequentially rather than allowing random round selection. No
  • Enable Feeding: This will allow the player to feed the pets after 3 battles (if area has exactly 5 rounds) or after 4 battles (if area has 6 or more rounds) following the DMX/PENZ pattern. Requires Battle Sequential Rounds to work. No

Care - Meat Settings

  • Weight Gain: Weight increase when feeding meat/hunger food. Applied only to pets above stage 1 and below 99 weight. 1
  • Hunger Gain: Amount of hunger restored per meat feeding. Pets can't eat when hunger equals stomach capacity or during overfeed timer. 1
  • Care Mistake Time: Minutes of empty hunger before triggering a care mistake. Timer resets when pet is fed. 10
  • Overfeed Timer: Minutes pet refuses food after trying to overfeed. Pet will display 'nope' animation during this time. 120
  • Condition Heart: When enabled, uses condition hearts system instead of mistake counter. Each care mistake reduces condition hearts. No
  • Can Eat Sleeping: Allows pets to eat food while in nap state. When disabled, feeding attempts during sleep will fail. Yes
  • Back to Sleep Time: Minutes before pet automatically returns to sleep after being woken up during sleep hours. 10
  • Enable Shaken Egg: Allows eggs to be "shaken" (shake_counter >= 99) for evolution bonuses. Can provide a power bonus. No
  • Flush Disturbance Sleep: When enabled, using flush while pet is sleeping counts as a sleep disturbance. No
  • Fixed 4 Hearts: When enabled, hunger and vitamin meters always show 4 hearts regardless of pet's stomach attribute. Yes
  • Poop Chance: Probability distribution for poop types (Single, Double, Triple, Giga). Percentages must sum to 100%. Single: 100%
  • Poop Alarm: When enabled, pet will call for attention when it needs to poop. Helps prevent care mistakes. No
  • 99g Effect: Defines what happens when a pet reaches 99g of weight. Nothing
  • Poop Sickness Count: How many poops on screen needed for the pet to get sick. 4
  • Poop Sickness Effect: Defines the visual effect when the pet gets sick from poop. Skull
  • Block Actions When Sleeping: When the pet is sleepy or asleep, it won't be possible to battle, feed or train. Yes
  • Can Battle While Sick: When enabled, the pet can battle even while sick. When disabled, battling is blocked until the pet is healed. No

Care - Protein Settings

  • Weight Gain: Weight increase when feeding protein/strength food. Applied only to pets above stage 1 and below 99 weight. 2
  • Strength Gain: Amount of strength restored per protein feeding. Strength depletes over time and empty strength triggers care mistakes. 1
  • DP Gain: DP (stamina) gained every 4th protein feeding if current DP is below max energy. Consumed during battles. 1
  • Care Mistake Time: Minutes of empty strength before triggering a care mistake. Timer resets when pet receives protein. 10
  • Overdose Max: Maximum protein overdose counter that affects battle sick chance. Each protein feeding increases overdose. 7
  • Protein Penalty: Determines the amount of each penalty for Protein Overdose in percent. Affects battle sick chance. 10
  • Disturbance Penalty Max: Maximum penalty from sleep disturbances that affects battle sick chance. Each disturbance adds 2 to penalty. 0
  • Sleep Care Mistake Timer: Minutes before triggering a care mistake for sleep neglect. Timer resets when pet gets proper sleep. 60

Death Settings

  • Max Injuries: Maximum injuries before death. Injuries are gained from sickness, poop neglect, and battle losses. 15
  • Care Mistake: Total care mistakes before death. Includes hunger, strength, sleep, and sickness mistakes. 0
  • Sick Timer: Minutes of continuous sickness before death. Timer only counts while sick status is active. 360
  • Hunger Timer: Minutes of empty hunger before death. Death occurs after continuous hunger neglect exceeding this threshold. 720
  • Starvation Count: Number of hunger depletion cycles before death. Each cycle occurs when hunger reaches 0 and isn't refilled. 0
  • Strength Timer: Minutes of empty strength before death. Represents weakness from lack of protein/training. 720
  • Stage 4-5 Mistake: Mistakes threshold for stage 4-5 pets after evolution time expires. Represents adult-stage care requirements. 5
  • Stage 6-7 Mistake: Mistakes threshold for stage 6+ pets after 48 hours of age. Expert care required for long-term survival. 5
  • Save by B Press: Enables death prevention by B button press. Gives 1 minute window to save pet from death conditions. No
  • Save by Shake: Enables death prevention by device shaking. Gives 1 minute window to save pet through physical interaction. No
  • Death by Old Age: Days of pet's life before death from old age. When set to 0, old age death is disabled. 0

Vital Values & Item Boosts

  • Vital Value Base: Minimum Vital Values gained per hour if well fed and not sick. Represents the natural recovery rate in good health. 0
  • Vital Value Loss: Vital Values lost per minute if hungry or sick. Higher values make pets deteriorate faster when neglected. 0
  • HP Max Item Boost: Maximum HP boost from items. 0 means only temporary boost items will work. 0
  • Attack Max Item Boost: Maximum attack boost from items. 0 means only temporary boost items will work. 0
  • Power Max Item Boost: Maximum power boost from items. 0 means only temporary boost items will work. 0

G-Cell Settings

  • Use G-Cells: Enables the G-Cell evolution system. G-Cells fluctuate based on care, training, battles, and protein feeding. No
  • Random Encounter Win: G-Cell points gained when winning random encounters. Default: +12. 0
  • Random Encounter Lose: G-Cell points lost when losing random encounters. Default: -6. 0
  • Battle Win: G-Cell points gained when winning regular battles. Default: +6. 0
  • Battle Lose: G-Cell points lost when losing regular battles. Default: -4. 0
  • Training Success: G-Cell points gained from successful training. Rewards training skill development. 0
  • Training Phase2 Failure: G-Cell points gained from advanced training failure. Default: +2. 0
  • Training Phase1 Failure: G-Cell points lost from basic training failure. Default: -2. 0
  • Protein: G-Cell points lost when feeding protein. Default: -4. 0
  • Care Mistake: G-Cell points lost per care mistake. Default: -8. Largest G-Cell penalty. 0
